# What to ask Bakırköy Belediyesi for

The goal is **one page, on letterhead, signed and dated**, that proves three
things to a stranger who has never heard of you:

1. A **named, specific** public sports facility — not "various fields in Bakırköy".
2. That you have **permission to install** equipment there.
3. That the **public may use it** — citizens, not staff, not a pilot group.

That is the whole job. It does not need to promise money, exclusivity, or a
long-term contract, and asking for less makes it far easier for them to sign
quickly. A one-line official email from a named `@bakirkoy.bel.tr` address is
worth more than a warm verbal promise, so take the email if the letterhead is
slow.

**Ask them for the two facts you need regardless of format:** the exact facility
name, and the date the unit can go on the wall. Those two go straight into
`data/stations.seed.ts` and into the Apple reply.

> **T.C. BAKIRKÖY BELEDİYE BAŞKANLIĞI**
> *[Müdürlük adı]*
>
> Sayı: …
> Tarih: … / … / 2026
>
> **Konu: Playbox spor ekipmanı istasyonu kurulumu hakkında**
>
> Bakırköy Belediyesi ile Canberk Varlı (Playbox) arasında, ilçemize bağlı
> **[TESİS ADI, örn. "Ataköy 7-8-9-10. Kısım Spor Sahası"]** adresinde,
> vatandaşlarımızın kullanımına açık bir spor ekipmanı kiralama istasyonunun
> kurulması hususunda mutabakata varılmıştır.
>
> İstasyon **[GG.AA.2026]** tarihi itibarıyla sahada kurulu olup, tesisi kullanan
> tüm vatandaşlarımız tarafından serbestçe kullanılabilecektir. Kurulum,
> işletme ve bakım sorumluluğu Canberk Varlı'ya aittir.
>
> Bilgilerinize sunulur.
>
> *[Ad Soyad]*
> *[Unvan — örn. Spor İşleri Müdürü]*
> *[İmza ve mühür]*

> *[Department]*
>
> Ref: …
> Date: … / … / 2026
>
> **Subject: Installation of a Playbox sports equipment station**
>

> public sports facility within our district, for use by members of the public.

> to all citizens using the facility. Installation, operation and maintenance are
> the responsibility of Canberk Varlı.
>
> *[Name]*
> *[Title]*
> *[Signature and official seal]*

## How this gets used with Apple

Attach it in **App Review Information** the same way you attached the 5.2.1
evidence, and reference it in the reply. It changes what you are: not a developer
with a prototype, but an operator with a municipal agreement and a fixed public
address. That is the direct answer to "designed only to demonstrate a concept".

**It is strongest once the unit is actually on the wall.** The letter says a
locker will be there; a photograph of it bolted up, plus the map pin matching the
facility address, proves it is. If you can only send one thing, send the photo of
the installed unit — but send both if you can.

## What NOT to do

- Do not send it before the station is mounted if the letter states a date that
  has already passed. A reviewer who checks the date against an empty map has
  caught you in a discrepancy, and this thread already has three rejections on it.
- Do not describe it as a pilot, trial, test, demo or launch event in either
  language. Those words map directly onto Guideline 2.2.
- Do not claim more stations than exist. One real station, precisely described,
  beats a vague plural every time.
