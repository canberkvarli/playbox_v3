# Guideline 5.2.1 — proving you are authorized to use "Playbox" + the hardware

Apple flagged this because the app uses the "Playbox" brand and controls
"Playbox" hardware, and your Developer account's legal name is not obviously the
same entity. You resolve it by attaching proof in the App Review Information
section. Attach as many of these as you have (more is better):

## Evidence checklist (attach any/all)
- [ ] Business / company registration for the entity that owns Playbox
      (Turkey: Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi or vergi levhası). Strongest single item.
- [ ] Trademark registration or application for "Playbox" / "Playbox Sport"
      (TÜRKPATENT filing receipt is fine if not yet granted).
- [ ] Proof you own the domain playboxsport.com (registrar receipt or WHOIS).
- [ ] The signed letter of authorization below (on letterhead, PDF).

If your Apple Developer account IS the company that owns Playbox, say so in the
reply and the registration document alone usually clears this. The letter below
matters most when the account holder (individual) differs from the brand owner.
